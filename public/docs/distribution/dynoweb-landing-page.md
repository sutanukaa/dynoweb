## Copy-safety checklist

- [ ] No banned words: `CRO` · `leaking funnel` · `nudge` · `attribution` · `autopilot` · `set and forget` · `AI CRO Expert`.
- [ ] Every feature = an outcome (lost sales, money made), never a capability (heatmap, replay).
- [ ] Money in the hero, the leak report, the proof, the close.
- [ ] Self-blame reduced, agency restored ("it's not you — one fixable thing").
- [ ] AI is always "you approve," never "it runs / auto-fixes."
- [ ] Verbs: `see · watch · spot · find`. Money: `makes money · recovered $X · save the sale`.
