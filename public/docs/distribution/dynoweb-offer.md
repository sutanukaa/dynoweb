## 0 · The one reframe the whole offer rests on

**Stop selling a $14 tool. Start selling a recovered sale — and gate the money on proof.**

The buyer (see ICP) is a broke, overwhelmed solo store owner who's been burned by apps, ads, and
advice, and is scared to spend again. They don't want a dashboard to operate. They want the leak
found and fixed. So the offer is **done-for-them and outcome-gated**: we find the leak free, deploy
the fix, they watch it work, and they only pay once it's made them money.

This reframe fits the buyer's real state *and* is the path off the two ceilings the honest read
named: the free-tool knife fight (Clarity is free) and the ~$209k self-serve ARR ceiling.

---

## 1 · Positioning statement

> **"We help Shopify store owners who get real traffic but not enough sales recover the money
> leaking out of their store — without a $5k agency, a six-week report, or touching a line of code."**

What is deliberately **absent**: "CRO", "heatmaps", "AI platform", "analytics". The buyer doesn't
search those and doesn't want a tool. Lead with the money; name the three fears (cost, delay, tech).

## 2 · The core outcome we sell

**Money made — recovered lost sales — with the anxiety of "I don't know what's wrong and I'm
bleeding money" removed.** Everything else (heatmaps, replays, the AI, the report) is *evidence*,
not the product. The line the buyer repeats: *"they found $4,000/month I was losing at checkout and
fixed it."*

Priority of outcomes: **money made → risk removed → time saved.** Never lead with time saved — for
a store owner in debt, money and fear beat convenience.

## 2.5 · What the offering is made of — the 4-act story

DynoWeb has 16 features. That is the problem, not the pitch: put all 16 on the page and it reads as
"another app with a lot of stuff" — the disease. The features only make a buyer say *"I want this"*
when they stop reading as a **menu** and start reading as a **story that happens to them**: dread →
you finally see it → it gets fixed → it pays you back → it keeps paying.

Four acts, in the order the buyer *feels* them. **Each act has one hero (what we lead with) and an
engine underneath (real, valuable, but never on the marquee).** Each act only earns the next.

### Act 1 — "See exactly where you're losing sales"
- **The dread it kills:** *"I've got traffic but no sales and I have no idea why. It looks fine to me."*
- **HERO → CRO Report** — the leak, as one dollar number: *"You're losing ~$4,200/mo, and here's the
  one place it's happening."* (Leads with **one** leak; the ranked suggestion list stays collapsed
  below — the suggestion engine is its brain, not a homework table.)
- **Engine under it →** Session Replay, Heatmaps, Journeys, Cart Overview, Page/Event Explorer,
  Traffic Sources.
- **The feeling:** the invisible becomes a number. *"Oh my god, THAT's where it's going."*

### Act 2 — "Fix it — no code, no dev, no waiting"
- **The dread it kills:** *"Even if I knew what was wrong, I've got no developer and nothing I try works."*
- **HERO → SmartNudge** — we deploy the fix *for* you (the cart-saver, the exit offer), live in
  minutes, and you approve it before it goes live.
- **Engine under it →** auto-discount creation, DynoAgent (drafts the copy/edit, *you* approve),
  SEO fixes.
- **The feeling:** relief. *"I don't have to figure it out or hire anyone. It just… gets fixed."*

### Act 3 — "Watch it make you money back"
- **The dread it kills:** *"I've been burned. Is any of this actually worth it, or another black hole?"*
- **HERO → Revenue Attribution ("the money")** — *"this fix recovered $1,400 last month,"* net, that
  you watch happen.
- **Engine under it →** the before/after replay clip, the money dashboard, SmartNudge per-fix stats.
- **The feeling:** payoff + trust. *"It paid for itself. I can SEE the sales it brought back."*

### Act 4 — "It keeps working while you sleep"
- **The dread it kills:** *"Tools go stale, I forget to log in, and I'm back where I started."*
- **HERO → accumulated history + the weekly signal** — *"here's what changed since last month, and
  the next fix."* (Clarity forgets after 30 days; we don't — philosophy P9.)
- **Engine under it →** weekly digest email, push alerts, trend/seasonal comparison.
- **The feeling:** it compounds. *"This isn't a one-time report — it's watching my store for me."*

> **The four acts ARE our loop — SEE → FIX → PROVE → STAY — which is the one thing no competitor
> does** (Clarity stops at SEE, popup apps stop at FIX, nobody closes it). The story and the
> differentiation are the same sentence.

### Priority list (what to lead with / build first)

**P0 — the loop. These three ARE the product; if they're not perfect, nothing else matters:**
1. CRO Report (SEE) · 2. SmartNudge (FIX) · 3. Revenue Attribution (PROVE)

**P1 — the engine. Real and valuable, but they *feed* a P0 — never the marquee:**
Session Replay · Heatmaps · Auto-discount · Weekly digest/alerts · Journeys / Cart Overview /
Traffic Sources / Explorers · Suggestions engine (the brain of the CRO Report — one leak up top,
list collapsed).

**P2 — waits. Off the story until the loop converts a paying stranger:**
DynoAgent chat · SEO Autopilot · AI image generation · PECTI / LIFT / health-grades (hide — jargon).

> **The one discipline, or it collapses back into a menu: one hero per act, on the marquee — the
> engine stays in the back room.** The moment all 16 features hit the page, the story dies. This map
> drives the pitch, the landing page (§4), and the app's nav — one source, three surfaces.

## 3 · Making ROI undeniable (three proofs)

1. **A dollar figure on the leak, before we ask for anything.** The CRO Report's revenue-leak
   estimate — *"you're losing ~$3,200–$5,800/month at the shipping step"* — computed on *their*
   store. Converts vague dread ("what am I doing wrong?") into a specific, quantified, fixable
   problem. This is the free hook.
2. **Recovered dollars, net, per fix.** SmartNudge attribution — *"this cart-saver recovered $1,400
   last month"* — the app visibly paying for itself. Against $14–29/mo, ROI is arithmetic they
   watch happen. Net of refunds, never gross (philosophy P8).
3. **They watch the before/after in a replay.** Deploy one fix, show the session recordings of
   shoppers who used to abandon and now don't. The **honest** proof (replay + attribution) — never
   a fabricated "we A/B-tested a 20% lift"; our market can't power that test and a savvy merchant
   catches the lie (P8). Watching a recovered checkout beats any stat.

## 4 · The entry offer (makes the first yes trivial)

> ### The Free Leak Report + First Fix Free
> *"We'll show you the single biggest reason you're losing sales — free. Then we deploy the fix for
> you and you watch it work. You only pay to keep it running once it's actually made you money."*

Why it's right for THIS buyer:
- **Zero money risk** — what a broke, burned merchant needs to hear first.
- **Done-for-them, not DIY** — no dev, no time; "we deploy it" beats "here are tools".
- **Outcome-gated** — payment triggers *after* attributed recovery. This one mechanic dissolves the
  #1 objection (§6).

**Mechanics:** free CRO report → deploy their #1 fix (usually a cart/exit SmartNudge) free for
14–30 days → attribution shows recovered sales → they convert to keep it live. Recovers nothing →
they owe nothing, and you've spent ~an hour.

> ⚠️ **Operational cost, stated plainly:** for early customers *you* (or a VA) hand-deploy the first
> fix. That doesn't scale to thousands — but with zero paying strangers today, hand-deploying the
> first 20–50 to *prove the motion converts* is exactly right. Automate it once it works, not before.

## 5 · Pricing — two layers, and the logic

**Layer 1 — self-serve, keep as-is: Free $0 · Growth $14 · Pro $29.**
The logic isn't "charge more" — **price was never the barrier.** At a recovered $1–4k/mo, $14–29 is
a 50–150× return. The tiers already sit right against the detectability floor (Growth's 7,500
sessions = the ICP band). The pricing *job* is to make the first recovered dollar **visible**, not
to raise the number. Trust and proof are the constraint, not price.

**Layer 2 — the real money: done-for-you / managed at ~$99–$299/mo.**
Where value-pricing actually applies and where we break the ~$209k self-serve ceiling. If we
recover $2–4k/mo *for* them, **$149/mo is a fraction of the value and a no-brainer** — and it's a
price a merchant *expects* to pay for "you handle it," vs. the $14 they expect for "a tool I run
myself." Same product underneath; we're selling the *deployment + monitoring*, which is what an
overwhelmed owner actually wants to buy.

> **Suggested launch:** done-for-you at **$149/mo, first cohort capped at 10–15.** Straight talk:
> the $14 self-serve is a real but small business; the $149 DFY is how this offer becomes worth real
> money — and it fits the buyer *better*, because they don't want a dashboard, they want the problem gone.

## 6 · The #1 objection, killed head-on

**The objection** (grounded in the review data + our zero-paying reality):
> *"I've already tried apps, ads, and advice — nothing worked, and I'm not spending more money on
> something that probably won't either."*

Three stacked fears — financial (in debt), burn (prior tools failed: *"took thousands of dollars,"
"money hungry," "use at your own risk"* — real competitor reviews), and trust (surprise-billed
before). Don't argue. Disarm all three in the offer itself:

- **Financial fear →** outcome-gated pricing: *"You don't pay a cent until you've watched it recover
  a sale."* Can't lose money on something you only pay for after it makes you money.
- **Prior-burn →** done-for-them + watched proof: *"You're not buying a promise — you're paying for
  a result you already saw in the replay."*
- **Trust/billing scar →** say the competitor wound out loud: *"Honest billing. No surprise charges,
  ever. Bots never count. And you approve every change — nothing happens to your store behind your
  back."* (that last clause also kills the AI-distrust wound — *"AI hallucinates and corrupts my store"*).

**The closing line:** *"You've been burned, so I've built the offer so you literally cannot be burned
again — you approve everything, and you don't pay until it's already made you money."*

## 7 · How to use this

- **Ad / listing CTA?** Resolve to the §4 entry offer ("Free Leak Report + First Fix Free"), not
  "Install now" or "Start free trial."
- **Outreach close?** Lead with the §3.1 dollar figure, offer §4, pre-empt with §6.
- **Pricing page / sales convo?** §5 — anchor the DFY tier on recovered dollars, not features.
- **Every claim** obeys philosophy P8: replay + attribution, net dollars, never a fabricated lift.
