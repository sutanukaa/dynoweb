Use this help center to understand how DynoWeb works and where to begin. DynoWeb is organized around five things you do — **Home, Fix, Nudges, Agent, and Explore** — so the docs are grouped the same way.

## Getting started

- [Overview](/help/overview) — what DynoWeb is and how it fits your store
- [Get Started](/help/getting-started) — install the tracker and connect your store

## Home

- [Dashboard](/help/dashboard) — your store's key metrics and behavior signals at a glance
- [Impact](/help/impact) — the money scoreboard: what every fix and nudge actually earned

## Fix

- [Suggestions](/help/suggestions) — prioritized, revenue-ranked improvements with implementation guides
- [CRO Report](/help/cro-report) — full store audits, KPI tracking, and prioritized recommendations
- [SEO Autopilot](/help/seo-autopilot) — AI-written SEO fixes applied in one click, fully reversible
- [Errors](/help/errors) — the JavaScript errors and broken 404s firing on your storefront, each with a plain-English fix

## Nudges

- [SmartNudge](/help/smartnudge) — behavior-triggered on-site nudges and coupon emails, styled to your brand and measured against a control group

## Agent

- [DynoAgent](/help/dynoagent) — your AI CRO expert: analyze your store and apply Shopify fixes with your approval

## Explore

- [Heatmaps](/help/heatmaps) — page-level click, scroll, and rage-click analysis
- [Replays](/help/replays) — session-level investigation of real shopper behavior
- [Journeys](/help/journeys) — entry pages, drop-off points, and common paths
- [Referrers](/help/referrers) — traffic sources by revenue, including AI-search (GEO) referrals
- [Conversions](/help/conversions) — connect tracked intent signals to orders and revenue
- [Cart Overview](/help/cart-overview) — product intent, cart activity, and pre-checkout drop-off

## Connect

- [MCP](/help/mcp) — plug DynoWeb into Claude.ai, Cursor, or ChatGPT as an MCP server

---

New here? Start with the [Overview](/help/overview), then [Get Started](/help/getting-started) to install the tracker. Once data is flowing, [Impact](/help/impact) keeps score, [Fix](/help/suggestions) and [SmartNudge](/help/smartnudge) do the work, [DynoAgent](/help/dynoagent) does it for you on request, and the [Explore](/help/heatmaps) surfaces show the evidence behind every recommendation.
