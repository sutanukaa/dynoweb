SEO Autopilot finds the SEO gaps across your entire Online Store and fixes them for you — with AI-written content, applied in one click, and fully reversible. No theme edits, no copy-pasting meta tags, no guesswork. You'll find it under **Fix → SEO**.

<img src="/SEOAutopilot.png" alt="SEO Autopilot panel listing products, collections, pages, and articles with missing SEO fields and one-click Apply buttons" />

## What it scans

SEO Autopilot audits your whole store — **products, collections, pages, and blog articles** — and flags where you're leaving search traffic on the table:

- Missing or weak **SEO page titles**
- Missing or weak **meta descriptions**
- Missing **image alt text**
- Missing **barcodes** (a deterministic SKU-to-barcode fix, no AI needed)

## How it fixes them

For each gap, SEO Autopilot **writes the fix with AI** — a clear, relevant title, meta description, or alt text tuned to that specific product or page — and lets you **apply it in one click**.

- **Applied through Shopify's official Admin API.** Changes are written directly to your product, collection, page, or article — never by editing theme files.
- **Fully reversible.** Don't like a change? Undo it. Every fix can be rolled back.
- **Fix in place.** SEO Autopilot appears right on the SEO suggestion details, so you can review and apply without hopping between screens.

## SEO and AI search (GEO)

Search is changing — shoppers increasingly find stores through AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, and similar) as well as Google. SEO Autopilot helps you cover the fundamentals that both rely on: clear titles, descriptions, and alt text.

Where a suggestion touches AI-search visibility (GEO), DynoWeb is upfront that these are still-emerging channels — it tells you what's within your control to fix and what depends on factors outside it, rather than overpromising rankings.

## FAQ

**Will this touch my theme or code?**
No. Every change is made through Shopify's Admin API on the product, collection, page, or article — never your theme files.

**Can I undo a change?**
Yes. Every fix is reversible.

**Does it write the content, or just tell me what's wrong?**
It writes the content for you and applies it on approval — you don't have to draft titles or descriptions yourself.

**Can DynoAgent do this too?**
Yes — you can ask DynoAgent to fix SEO on specific products, and it will prepare the changes for your approval.
