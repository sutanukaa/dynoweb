Impact is DynoWeb's scoreboard. It answers the one question every merchant actually cares about: **"What has this app made me?"**

Every fix you apply and every nudge you launch is tracked here, measured against how your store was performing before, and reported as real money — so you never have to wonder what DynoWeb did for you. You'll find it under **Home → Impact**.

## What Impact shows

**The money, first.** At the top is a running total of the revenue DynoWeb has earned or saved you — combining verified fixes and the net revenue your SmartNudge campaigns produced. It's the number that matters, kept front and center.

**Every fix, measured honestly.** Each change is judged on the metric that actually fits it:

- A CTA or button fix is measured on **click-through rate**.
- A frustration fix is measured on **rage clicks**.
- An SEO fix is measured on **organic visitors**.
- A GEO / AI-search fix is measured by whether it's **verified live**.

**Before → after, with the window.** Each item shows how its metric moved from before the change to after, along with the exact measurement period — so you can see the timeframe behind every result.

**Compared against your store's own trend.** Impact doesn't just credit DynoWeb for changes that would have happened anyway. It uses a **difference-in-differences** approach — comparing the change against your store's overall trend over the same period — so the number reflects the fix's real contribution, not background noise.

## Still-measuring items

Some fixes take time to gather enough data for a confident result. Rather than showing a blank "Measuring…", Impact displays the **real data collected so far** against the baseline snapshot captured when the change went live — so you can watch a result build instead of staring at an empty row.

## Honest by design

Impact follows two rules:

- **Net, not gross.** SmartNudge revenue is reported after refunds and after subtracting the control group, so the total reflects money you actually kept.
- **Estimates, never guarantees.** Every number is a labelled, measured estimate — DynoWeb tells you what it observed and over what window, and never dresses an estimate up as a promise.

## FAQ

**How does DynoWeb know a change caused the result?**
It compares each fix's metric against your store's overall trend for the same period (difference-in-differences), so normal ups and downs don't get miscredited.

**Why is one of my fixes still "measuring"?**
It hasn't gathered enough data yet for a confident before-and-after. Impact shows the data collected so far in the meantime.

**Does the total include my SmartNudge campaigns?**
Yes — the headline combines verified fixes with the net incremental revenue from your nudges.
