SmartNudge is where DynoWeb crosses the last mile: instead of just telling you a fix, it **deploys** it. You build behavior-triggered on-site messages — popups, bars, wheels, quizzes, and more — that recover abandoning shoppers, grow your email list, and lift average order value. Every nudge is styled to your brand automatically, and every nudge is measured against a control group so you know the real revenue it earned.

## What it does

SmartNudge has two connected parts.

The **intervention list** shows every nudge you've created, its status (active, paused, or draft), and its performance — impressions, clicks, and the net revenue it produced. AI-suggested nudges also appear here for you to review; nothing fires on your storefront until you activate it.

The **builder** is a live-preview workspace. As you move through **Template → Content → Trigger → Delivery → Launch**, a preview of your nudge updates in real time beside you, so you always see exactly what shoppers will see.

## Brand DNA — nudges that look like your store

Before you design anything, SmartNudge can learn your brand. **Brand DNA** crawls representative pages of your storefront and extracts:

- Your **color palette** (background, text, headline, CTA, accent, and more)
- Your **fonts** (headline and body, matched to fonts that actually load on your store)
- Your **brand personality and voice** (tone, adjectives, words to use and avoid)
- Your **imagery style** and logo

Extraction takes around 30 seconds. From then on, every nudge you build inherits these design tokens, so it looks native to your store out of the box — no manual styling required. You can save multiple brand profiles per store and edit any field after extraction.

## Building a nudge

Open the builder and you'll move through five steps, with a live preview updating beside you the whole time:

1. **Template** — start from a ready-made template or a blank component type.
2. **Content** — write your headline, body, and CTA (or generate them with AI), pick or generate an image, and apply your Brand DNA and design.
3. **Trigger** — choose who sees the nudge and when: a visitor segment (exit intent, cart abandoner, price hesitator, high-intent browser, and more), plus frequency and target pages.
4. **Delivery** — choose how it reaches shoppers: on-site, and — when the nudge captures an email — a follow-up coupon email (see below).
5. **Launch** — review and activate.

## Delivery: on-site and email

SmartNudge reaches shoppers two ways:

- **On-site** — the nudge renders on your storefront through DynoWeb's lightweight widget, triggered by real behavior (exit intent, scroll, time on page, cart value, and more).
- **Email** — when a shopper enters their email in a nudge, DynoWeb can send them a **coupon email that inherits the same design** as the popup, so the follow-up feels like a natural continuation of the offer.

## Attribution — proof, not guesswork

This is what sets SmartNudge apart. Every nudge runs against a **control group** (a default 10% holdout who don't see it). SmartNudge then compares the two groups and reports:

- **Lift** — the difference in conversion rate between shoppers who saw the nudge and the holdout.
- **Incremental revenue** — the *net* extra revenue the nudge produced, after normalizing the holdout to the same size and subtracting it out.

To keep things honest, SmartNudge waits until there's enough data before showing a precise percentage — below that threshold it tells you the result is still forming rather than quoting a number it can't stand behind. You'll find this under **Nudges → Attribution**.

## A/B testing

Run two variants head-to-head — different copy, component, or trigger timing. You set the traffic split, DynoWeb assigns visitors, and a statistical test recommends the winner once results are significant. Use A/B tests to validate a clear hypothesis, not to go fishing.

## The Suggestion → Nudge loop

DynoWeb connects the problem to the fix. When a revenue leak surfaces in your Suggestions or CRO Report, you can answer it directly with a nudge. Once that nudge starts winning against its holdout, the original leak is marked **"acted on — working,"** and DynoWeb shows you the money it earned.
