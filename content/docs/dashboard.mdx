Home answers three questions, in order: **what did DynoWeb earn me, how healthy is my store, and what should I do next.** It opens on **This month** so the numbers here line up with the session count on your plan.

## Greeting Bar

The top of Home tells you the app is working and gives you the controls for everything below it.

Alongside the greeting you'll see a **Tracker live** badge — if it reads **Tracker paused**, DynoWeb isn't collecting anything and nothing below will update. Next to it sits the date-range picker (Today, Yesterday, This month, Last 7 / 30 / 90 days), a **DynoAgent** button that opens your AI CRO expert, and the notification bell.

<img src="/home-greeting.png" alt="Home greeting bar with the tracker status badge, date range picker, DynoAgent button, and notification bell" />

## Notifications

The bell collects the changes worth your attention — a spike in errors, a drop worth investigating, a nudge worth reviewing — so you don't have to go hunting for them.

Each alert carries a severity (Critical, Warning, or Update), a plain-English detail, and a link straight to the surface that can fix it. Mark them done individually or clear them all at once. When there's nothing to report, it says so rather than inventing noise.

<img src="/notifications.png" alt="Notification panel showing alerts with severity badges, plain-English detail, and a direct action link on each" />

## Banner

Directly under the greeting, Home shows **one** banner — never a stack. It's whichever thing matters most right now, in this order: storefront errors that could be blocking checkout, then your session limit (at the cap, then approaching it), then new high-impact suggestions waiting for review.

Banners aren't dismissible, because each one clears itself the moment the underlying problem does.

## Money Earned

This is the headline: **what DynoWeb made you this month**, in your currency, above everything else on the page.

The caption splits the total into SmartNudge revenue and verified fixes, and **View breakdown** opens the full [Impact](/help/impact) scoreboard. Every figure here is an estimate measured against your own trend — never a guarantee. If the total is still zero, the card says so plainly and tells you to deploy a nudge or apply a fix to start attributing value.

<img src="/home-money-hero.png" alt="Home money hero showing what DynoWeb earned this month, split between SmartNudge and verified fixes" />

## Store Pulse

Four tiles give the fastest read on the store, each with a sparkline of its real daily trend and an up/down badge against the previous period.

**Attributed sales**, **Sessions**, **Conversion rate**, and **revenue at risk** (the value sitting in abandoned carts). Each tile is clickable and drops you into the matching Explore surface — sales and conversion rate into [Conversions](/help/conversions), sessions into [Replays](/help/replays), and revenue at risk into [Cart Overview](/help/cart-overview). Before there's data, each tile says what it's waiting for instead of showing a zero.

<img src="/home-store-pulse.png" alt="Store pulse tiles: attributed sales, sessions, conversion rate, and revenue at risk, each with a sparkline and trend badge" />

## Store Health

A single score out of 100 built from the signals DynoWeb already has on your store, graded **Healthy**, **Good**, **Needs review**, or **At risk**.

It's made of four factors, each one clickable so you can go straight to the problem: **Performance** (how fast the store feels), **Errors** (JavaScript errors firing on your storefront), **SEO** (issues waiting to be fixed), and **Experience** (how much frustration your sessions show). Factors only appear once there's signal behind them — a store with no replays yet won't show Experience.

This is a quick live read, deliberately simpler than the full audit. For the graded, benchmarked version, open the [CRO Report](/help/cro-report) from the card.

<img src="/home-store-health.png" alt="Store Health card with the score donut and the Performance, Errors, SEO, and Experience factors" />

## What DynoWeb Is Doing For You

Below the health score, one card per capability — each showing what it's done lately and the single next action worth taking.

**SmartNudge** shows live nudges with what they've been shown, converted, and earned; with no nudges running, it shows how many visitors came back without buying and offers to win them back. **Suggestions** shows what's open and how many are high-impact. **SEO**, **Storefront health**, and **Replays** appear once there's something to report — SEO issues to fix, errors caught this week, or sessions worth watching.

Cards you don't need stay hidden. That's intentional: the grid only surfaces work that actually exists.

<img src="/home-capability-cards.png" alt="Capability cards for SmartNudge, Suggestions, SEO, storefront health, and replays, each with its metrics and next action" />

## Explore Your Store

Two cards close out the page: where your visitors are, and how fast the store feels to them.

**Visitor Locations** maps sessions by country with attributed revenue alongside, and expands to a fullscreen view with state, region, and city-level drill-down where available.

**Storefront speed** leads with a verdict, not a number — *Your store is fast*, *A few pages are dragging*, or *Speed is costing you sales* — so you know whether to care before reading anything. Below it are your four **Core Web Vitals** measured from real visitors, the same way Google grades you: Loading (LCP), Responsiveness (INP), Visual stability (CLS), and Server response (TTFB). **Loading speed over time** charts your daily trend, and **Slow pages worth your attention** ranks the pages that are dragging by the real revenue flowing through them — so you fix the slow pages that cost you money first. From there, **View performance fixes** takes you to the matching suggestions. On a new or low-traffic store, the card says there isn't enough data yet rather than inventing numbers.

## Plan & Usage

The strip at the bottom shows sessions used against your monthly cap, with a progress bar.

Only real shopper sessions count toward the limit — bots and your own admin traffic don't. This is the same number the [pricing page](/help/overview) uses, so the two never disagree.

<img src="/home-plan-strip.png" alt="Plan and usage strip showing sessions used against the monthly cap" />

## Going Deeper Into Pages

Home deliberately doesn't carry page-by-page tables any more. For the full per-page picture — traffic, engagement, scroll depth, revenue, orders, conversion rate, replay activity, health score, and related suggestions — open **Page Explorer** under Explore.

Sort it by **Most Problematic** to see the pages needing attention first, or **Most Traffic** to see where engagement is going.
